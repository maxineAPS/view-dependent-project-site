How to use it

  1. Views. Each card is one viewing direction and the image you want to see from it. Pick an image from the list or upload your own. Use two to four views, at least 40° apart.
  2. Step 1: Optimize. Runs the optimizer. The chart shows the loss for each view.
  3. Step 2: Project. Projects each image onto the sides of the bars its camera sees, and builds a closed, textured mesh.
  4. Step 3: Download printable mesh. Saves the mesh (OBJ, MTL and PNG) and the optimized heights and colours, which reproject.py can read.
  5. Look from view on a card shows the surface from that camera.

Everything runs in your browser, and your images are not uploaded. We ported the code from Python to JavaScript for this page. The method is explained in the walkthrough.
